Why a fixed payment matters

A fixed monthly amount does not shrink automatically as the balance falls. If the payment is comfortably above monthly interest, the principal reduction normally accelerates over time.

How much should you pay?

The calculator does not prescribe a payment. It shows the mathematical consequence of the amount you choose. Compare the base payment with modest increases before deciding what fits your cash flow.

Important assumption

No new purchases or fees are added. Actual card finance charges can be calculated daily and taxes or other charges can change the result.

Common questions

What happens if my payment is lower than monthly interest?

The balance will not amortize in this simplified model. The calculator flags that situation instead of showing a misleading payoff date.

Does paying more always save interest?

In this fixed-rate, no-new-spending model, paying principal sooner reduces the balance on which future interest is calculated.
