What compounding does

Each compounding period adds earned interest to the balance, so later interest can be earned on earlier interest.

Frequency

Enter 1 for annual, 2 for half-yearly, 4 for quarterly or 12 for monthly compounding.

Nominal rate assumption

The input is treated as a nominal annual rate divided equally across compounding periods.
