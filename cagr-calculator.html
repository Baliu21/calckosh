What CAGR means

CAGR is the constant annual compound rate that would connect the starting and ending values over the selected period.

It hides volatility

Two investments can have the same CAGR but very different year-to-year paths.

Useful comparison

CAGR is useful for comparing long-term growth across different time periods.
