What EMI means

EMI is the fixed monthly instalment used to repay principal and interest on a reducing-balance loan. A longer tenure normally reduces the monthly EMI but increases total interest.

Formula used

The calculator uses the standard reducing-balance annuity formula with the annual rate converted to a monthly rate.

Reality check

Lender schedules can differ slightly because of rounding, disbursement dates, rate resets, fees and product-specific rules.
