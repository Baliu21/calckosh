What EMI actually tells you

EMI is useful for monthly affordability, but it is not the full cost of borrowing. The same loan amount can have a lower EMI simply because the tenure is longer, which can increase total interest substantially.

Why an extra monthly payment changes the result

Any amount paid above the scheduled EMI is modelled as additional principal reduction. That smaller balance produces less interest in later months and can shorten the loan.

Formula and assumptions

The calculation uses the standard reducing-balance annuity formula with the annual rate converted to a monthly rate. It assumes a constant rate, monthly payments and no lender fees or rate resets.

Common questions

Does a lower EMI mean a cheaper loan?

No. A longer tenure can lower EMI while increasing total interest.

Is extra monthly payment the same as a prepayment?

Mathematically both reduce principal earlier, but lender rules can differ. Use the separate prepayment calculator for a one-time lump sum.
