Compound growth is nonlinear

The effect of compounding becomes more visible as the holding period grows because each period's return is applied to prior gains as well as the original investment.

Do not treat the entered return as a promise

Market-linked assets do not compound at one fixed rate in real life. Use several scenarios to understand sensitivity rather than relying on one output.

Nominal versus real purchasing power

This page does not subtract inflation. A future rupee amount can be larger while having less purchasing power than the nominal figure suggests.

Common questions

Is the future value a forecast?

No. It is the mathematical result of applying the entered constant annual return. Real market returns are variable and can be negative.

Why do small return differences matter over long periods?

Compounding repeatedly applies each year's gain to an increasingly large base, so rate differences widen over time.

Does the calculator include tax, fees or inflation?

No. It models nominal compound growth only. Use the inflation calculator separately to explore purchasing-power effects.
