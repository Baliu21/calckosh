How CalcKosh is designed

Useful results before unnecessary complexity.

See the cost, not only the headline number.

Loan and debt tools expose modeled interest, repayment time and comparison scenarios.

Treat investment returns as scenarios.

Market-linked calculators clearly separate assumptions from guarantees and show sensitivity where useful.

Keep the calculation portable.

Supported high-value calculators let you share scenarios, copy results and print them without an account.
