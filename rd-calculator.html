Why RD growth differs from an FD

An FD usually places the full principal at work from the beginning. An RD adds money gradually, so later installments have less time to earn interest.

What the chart separates

The deposited line is the money you contributed. The estimated-value line adds modeled growth, making it easier to see how much of the final amount comes from your own deposits.

Planning, not a bank quotation

Use this for savings planning. Confirm exact maturity values with the institution because product rules can differ.

Common questions

Is this the exact maturity amount my bank will pay?

Not necessarily. Actual RD products can use institution-specific compounding, installment dates, day counts and rounding.

Does the calculator assume I miss any installments?

No. It assumes every monthly deposit is made on schedule.

Why is the investment gain smaller in the early years?

Later installments have less time to earn interest, so recurring deposits compound differently from investing the whole amount on day one.
