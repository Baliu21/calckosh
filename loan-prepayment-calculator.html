Why timing matters

Interest is calculated on the outstanding principal. A prepayment made earlier leaves a smaller balance for more future months, so the same rupee amount usually has a larger interest-saving effect earlier in the loan.

What this calculator assumes

The scheduled EMI and annual interest rate remain unchanged after the lump-sum payment. The benefit is therefore shown mainly as a shorter tenure. Real lenders may instead offer a lower EMI or require a specific prepayment instruction.

Check lender rules before acting

Fees, minimum prepayment amounts and product-specific restrictions are not included. Confirm the lender's current terms before making a payment.

Common questions

Should I reduce EMI or tenure after prepayment?

Reducing tenure generally saves more interest if the payment amount stays affordable, but the right choice depends on your cash-flow needs.

Why does an early prepayment save more?

Because principal is reduced for a larger number of future interest periods.
