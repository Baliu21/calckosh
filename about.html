About CalcBharat

CalcBharat is a lightweight collection of free browser-based calculators for everyday decisions. The project intentionally avoids logins, unnecessary data collection and server-side complexity. Current calculators focus on transparent deterministic formulas rather than personalized financial advice.

Simple by design

No calculator result should require an account. We aim to keep the site lightweight, fast and understandable.
