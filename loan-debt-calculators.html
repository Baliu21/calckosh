Start with the decision you are actually making

Use the EMI calculator when affordability and total loan cost are the question. Use loan comparison when choosing between offers. Use prepayment when you already have a loan and are considering reducing principal.

For credit cards, payment size matters

Minimum-due calculations can make debt look manageable month to month while keeping a balance alive for much longer. Compare fixed payment amounts and total modeled interest.

Planning estimates are not lender statements

Real lenders can use different fee, compounding, rounding, rate-reset and prepayment rules. Treat CalcKosh as a transparent planning model and confirm contractual figures with the lender.
