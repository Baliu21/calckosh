Do not compare only the headline rate

A loan with a lower interest rate can still cost more if its tenure is much longer or its fees are higher. Compare EMI for affordability and total repayment for cost.

What the fee field means

Enter known upfront charges you want included in the comparison. This is a simple rupee addition and does not attempt to calculate APR or the timing effect of financed fees.

How to use the result

Use the cheaper-total indicator as a screening tool, then compare the lender's official disclosures, prepayment rules, reset clauses and insurance requirements.

Common questions

Can the lower-EMI loan cost more overall?

Yes. A longer tenure can reduce EMI while increasing the number of months on which interest is paid.

Are processing fees included?

Only if you enter them in the upfront-fee fields. Other charges and taxes are not automatically added.
