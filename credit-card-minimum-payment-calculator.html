Why paying only the minimum can take years

A percentage-based minimum usually falls as the outstanding balance falls. That makes the payment easier each month, but it can also slow principal reduction. A fixed rupee payment keeps more pressure on the balance and can cut both time and interest substantially.

How this model works

Each month the calculator applies the entered monthly interest rate to the opening balance, calculates the payment, and carries the remainder forward. It assumes no new purchases, cash advances, late fees or other charges.

Do not treat this as your bank statement

Indian card issuers can calculate finance charges daily and minimum amount due rules differ by issuer and account. Use this tool to understand the direction and scale of the cost, then confirm the exact amount from your statement.

What to look for

If a fixed payment is below the monthly interest, the debt cannot amortize in this simplified model. If it is comfortably above interest, compare the interest saved rather than looking only at the monthly payment.

Common questions

Does paying the minimum due avoid interest?

No. Paying the minimum can help keep the account from being treated as unpaid, but revolving balances can still attract finance charges under the issuer's terms.

Why can a fixed payment clear debt faster?

Because the payment does not automatically fall with the balance. More of the payment can go toward principal as interest falls.

Should I use the monthly or annual rate?

This page asks for the monthly rate shown in, or derived from, your card pricing. The separate payoff calculator also supports a fixed-payment view.
